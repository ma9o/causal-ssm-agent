## 2026-04-22 — Wednesday, 9:15 PM

Saw Dr. Patel today. Asked him about coming off.

He didn't say no. He didn't say yes. He said the things doctors say when they want you to make a real decision: numbers, timelines, the word "hyperbolic" applied to a taper schedule which, in 2019, would have struck me as ridiculous and, now, sounds like an act of grace.

Recurrence rate first year off: 25-35%. On: 10-15%. He said this lightly because it is a number. Numbers are not the question.

The question is: what part of me is the medication? Two and a half years in and Mom getting sick and not falling apart and Sam staying — which version of me did all of that, the medicated one or the underlying one or some braid the lexicon doesn't have a word for. I don't know. The doctor doesn't know. Helen doesn't know. Helen has been saying — for months now — that I should write down what "better" would actually look like, separately from what I'm afraid of giving up. I haven't done that.
