### Discussion of Discontinuation

Reviewed with patient:

1. **Recurrence risk for first-episode MDD treated with SSRI** is approximately 25–35% within 1 year of discontinuation; higher within 6 months of taper. This is in contrast to a continued-treatment recurrence rate of ~10–15% per year.
2. **Discontinuation syndrome risk:** Escitalopram has a moderate half-life (~27–32 h, longer in CYP2C19 intermediate metabolizers per patient's prior 23andMe data which she has shared). Risk of withdrawal symptoms is intermediate — lower than paroxetine or venlafaxine, higher than fluoxetine. Hyperbolic taper substantially reduces risk.
3. **Hyperbolic taper plan** (Horowitz & Taylor, *Lancet Psychiatry* 2019): proportional reductions over months, not weeks. Proposed schedule shared with patient (10 mg x 4 weeks → 5 mg x 4 weeks → 2.5 mg x 4 weeks → 1.25 mg x 4 weeks → 0). Total taper duration ~16 weeks.
4. **Stress timing:** Recommended initiating taper during a period of relative life stability. Patient has no major stressors anticipated for the next 6 months. Mother completing adjuvant therapy in May.
5. **Monitoring plan:** Self-monitor PHQ-9 weekly via Apple Health. Contact office if score >9 sustained x2 weeks, or any SI emergence. Would resume continuation dose at any point if symptoms re-emerge.

Patient is interested but ambivalent. Recognizes this is not directly answerable from clinical data alone.
